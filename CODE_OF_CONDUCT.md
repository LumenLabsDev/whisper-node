# Code of Conduct

This project adopts the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

## Our standards

We want whisper-node to be a welcoming project for everyone. In issues, pull requests, discussions and any other project space:

- Be respectful and constructive, especially when disagreeing.
- Focus on the problem, not the person.
- Accept feedback gracefully and give it with care.
- Do not harass, insult or demean anyone, and do not publish others' private information.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove comments, commits, issues and other contributions that break these rules, and may temporarily or permanently ban contributors who do so.

Report unacceptable behavior to **maximiliano.veiga@lumenlabs.dev**. Reports are reviewed promptly and kept confidential.

The full text of the Contributor Covenant, including its enforcement guidelines, applies to this project.
